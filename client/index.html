Multicam by keystrokes experimental trial

Premiere has no script command to switch a multicam angle, so PodDirector presses your Cut to Camera shortcuts for you. To try it: open a sequence with a multicam clip on it (ideally a duplicate), park the playhead where the trial should start, click Run trial, then click once in the Timeline during the countdown (a yellow bar at the top counts down). Don't touch the mouse or keyboard until it finishes; switching to another window stops it.
